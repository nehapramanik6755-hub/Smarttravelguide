Apple Country Resort Manali

Log Huts Area, Manali, Himachal Pradesh

Apple Country Resort Manali is a charming four-star mountain resort surrounded by apple orchards and breathtaking Himalayan views. Located close to Hadimba Temple and Mall Road, the resort offers spacious rooms, modern amenities, delicious dining, and warm hospitality. It is an ideal destination for families, couples, honeymooners, and nature lovers looking for a peaceful stay in Manali.
