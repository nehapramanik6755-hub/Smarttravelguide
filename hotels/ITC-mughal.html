ITC Mughal Agra

Fatehabad Road, Agra, Uttar Pradesh

ITC Mughal Agra is a luxurious five-star hotel inspired by Mughal architecture. Surrounded by beautiful gardens, the hotel offers spacious rooms, fine dining restaurants, a luxury spa, modern amenities, and world-class hospitality. It is an ideal destination for families, couples, and business travelers seeking a premium stay in Agra.
