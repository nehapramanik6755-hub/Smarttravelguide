The Leela Kovalam, A Raviz Hotel

Beach Road, Kovalam, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala

The Leela Kovalam, A Raviz Hotel is a luxury five-star beachfront resort located on a scenic cliff overlooking the Arabian Sea. The resort offers elegant rooms, sea-view villas, fine dining restaurants, a world-class spa, multiple swimming pools, and warm hospitality. It is an ideal destination for families, couples, honeymooners, and business travelers seeking a relaxing stay in Kerala.
