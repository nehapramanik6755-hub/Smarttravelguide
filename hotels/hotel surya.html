Hotel Surya Varanasi

S.20/51, The Mall Road, Cantonment, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Hotel Surya Varanasi is a charming heritage hotel located in the Cantonment area of Varanasi. Built around a historic palace, the hotel combines traditional elegance with modern comfort. It offers spacious rooms, lush gardens, an outdoor swimming pool, fine dining, and warm hospitality, making it an ideal choice for families, couples, business travelers, and pilgrims visiting the holy city.
