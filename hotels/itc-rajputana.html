ITC Rajputana Jaipur

Palace Road, Gopalbari, Jaipur, Rajasthan

ITC Rajputana Jaipur is a luxury five-star hotel inspired by the traditional havelis of Rajasthan. Located in the heart of Jaipur, the hotel offers elegant rooms, award-winning restaurants, a luxury spa, a swimming pool, and exceptional hospitality. It is an excellent choice for families, couples, and business travelers seeking a royal stay in the Pink City.
