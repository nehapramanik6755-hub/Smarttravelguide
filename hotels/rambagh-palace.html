Rambagh Palace Jaipur

Bhawani Singh Road, Jaipur, Rajasthan

Rambagh Palace Jaipur is a world-famous luxury heritage hotel that was once the residence of the Maharaja of Jaipur. Surrounded by beautiful gardens, the palace offers royal suites, fine dining restaurants, a luxury spa, and exceptional hospitality, making it one of India's most iconic destinations for luxury travelers.
